Our Dinner Rotation

Familiar favourites, two new recipes and small nutrition upgrades.

Two new recipes

1. Soba, tofu & edamame salad

Try the BBC Good Food recipe ↗. Tofu and edamame add soy protein; cucumber, beansprouts, spring onion and citrus-soy dressing bring variety. Keep dressing separate for leftovers; use reduced-salt tamari and leave chilli out for Jasper.

2. Roasted vegetable, chickpea & bulgur bowls

For 4: 2 tins chickpeas, 250–300g bulgur, 2 peppers, 1 courgette, 1 red onion, olive oil, cumin, paprika, lemon, plain yoghurt and tahini; herbs optional.

Method: Roast drained chickpeas and chopped vegetables with oil and spices at 180°C fan for 25–35 minutes. Cook bulgur to packet instructions. Mix yoghurt, tahini, lemon and a little water for a dressing. Serve together with herbs. Refrigerate leftovers promptly.

Simple nutrition principles

  • Add beans, lentils, chickpeas, tofu or edamame to most dinners where they fit.
  • Use wholewheat pasta, wholemeal wraps, brown rice, bulgur or quinoa when you enjoy them; mix with familiar grains at first.
  • Build variety through vegetables of different colours, plus nuts and seeds across the week.
  • Pair iron-rich pulses and greens with vitamin-C-rich peppers, tomatoes or lemon.
  • For a vegetarian family, keep an eye on iron, B12, calcium, iodine and vitamin D across the whole diet; check fortified foods and age-appropriate guidance.
  • For Jasper, chop nuts appropriately; avoid whole nuts because of choking risk, and keep salty sauces and added salt modest.

This is a dinner rotation, not a guarantee that the whole diet is nutritionally complete; breakfasts, lunches, snacks, portions and fortified foods also matter.
